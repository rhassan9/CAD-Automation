Bill of Materials (BOM) / Deliverables Generation
- Type: Excel/CAD Integration Script
- Goal: Auto-populate deliverables spreadsheet from CAD file data
- Current Status: Partially automated; cable sheet still requires manual work. Master prompts are used to fill in the 'house count' sheet, the '1x8 splitter' sheet, and the '1x8 to 1x4 splitter' sheet. They are inconsistent.

'House count' is a sheet that includes all the addresses of our houses and businesses that we are servicing. This sheet would take a decent amount of time to manually populate; but the master prompt we use does not appear to be consistent in output despite numerous attempts.

The 1x8 splitter sheet is relatively simple to understand and potentially automate by pulling data directly from the 1x8 splitter blocks within autocad

<<second image>>

The block itself contains the name and address, which also includes the fiber allocated (113 in example) to this specific splitter - this number basically equals the column# within this sheet. From there every column else is data entry.

<<third image>>>
The 1x8 to 1x4 splitter sheet is going to be a bit more difficult to automate based upon your knowledge of fiber, but I intend on writing out something detailed to explain what you're looking at.

Essentially, every 1x8 feeds 8 1x4's. And each 1x4 feeds 4 service houses/businesses. The heavy lift of this sheet is matching the 1x4's to each of its (max) 4 service location addresses.

<<fourth image>>

After that sheet is filled out, we use the data from there to use our master prompt and populate the house count sheet with the data from in there.

'Labor span sheet' is one that we have no current automation for. It appears to be a near match to the 'cable sheet'.
